# Research Portfolio 

This repository has been used to host my research/personal portfolio. Within it, you may find two branches representing older versions of my portfolio, versions that were vibecoded from A to Z. I decided to redo my portfolio from scratch in this branch, typing every single line of code.

Since no one will read this README.md, I'm going to use it as a personal journaling page about my own reflections when it comes to employability in the field of CS. I have been motivated to rework on my portfolio after hearing this analogy that resonated with me : For your portfolio, you're building something of quality, not quantity. It's like if you wanted to be an apprentice to the best weaver in the world, they've been weaving all their life and have gotten good at their craft through continuous learning and trial and error, and you're like : Yo, please take me as an apprentice, here look at these cheap blankets I mass-produced in a factory. 

A starting point in my opinion is the portfolio website, if you vibe coded your own portfolio website then how do you want a recruiter, or anyone who knows a thing or two about coding, that the projects within it are your own work?
